# PR9 — the GPUI Desktop: the first graphical session, attention, and control surface

## Prerequisites (grill Q9) and status

S6 merged (#43), Q6 merged (#46), the deny policy merged (#44), ADR 0017
accepted (#48) and implemented (#49). The structural decisions below were
ruled 2026-09-05 (`docs/decisions/2026-09-05-pr9-plan-grill.md`, two
rounds), and the reconciliation pass those rulings required ran against
`main` at `fc61325`. It found only faithful materialization:

- The frame kinds are `Geometry` (7) and `Palette` (8), the capabilities
  `terminal.geometry.v1` and `terminal.palette.v1` — the names this plan
  uses.
- The replica rules in D2 are ADR 0017 D1 and D4 as accepted: one state
  point per prefix, a `Snapshot` without its `Geometry` under the
  capability is a desync, a stale epoch's prefix is discarded, a resync is
  answered by the full prefix. D3's checkpoint is applied as bytes; its
  reset form (OSC 104/110/111) is one qwertty-term-vt 0.4.0 already
  handles.
- The merged channel — a writer task of its own, a 2 s no-progress
  deadline, a superseded backlog replaced by a fresh prefix — asks one
  thing of a client: keep reading the socket. The bridge's reader task
  does, off the UI thread. *Amended 2026-09-05 (post-merge review of #51,
  finding 1): reading past what the window has room for turned corrald's
  per-viewer budget into unbounded Desktop memory. The reader reads as far
  as the window's room, so the daemon's own budget supersedes the backlog;
  a window that takes nothing for 2 s loses the channel, as any client
  does.*
- `deny.toml` as merged passes `cargo deny check` over the gpui 0.2.2 tree
  (advisories, bans, licenses, sources).

No new decision. The plan is **accepted**; implementation proceeds from it
without another grill.

## Goal

The Desktop the spike grill's Q12 scoped: a GPUI application that shows
every session with the PR8 five-state projection, opens one into a terminal
view rendered from a client-side replica, and offers the M1 control
actions the accepted ladder allows — New Session, Open/Attach, input,
interrupt, detach, Continue in Corral with its disclosure, acknowledge.
It consumes the daemon's session, attention, and terminal truth through
`corral-client` and invents no runtime model of its own (`AGENTS.md`
§Client / daemon boundary).

## Non-goals (Q12)

No tray, no native notifications, no history/transcript browser, no
packaging or installer, no new provider capabilities. Linux rendering stays
unvalidated and says so in the support matrix; Linux compile and
platform-independent tests are in. No wire change: ADR 0017 completed what
a replica client needs.

## Existing owner / architecture involved

- `corral-client`: activation and the RPC surface every surface uses
  (`session_list`, `attention_*`, `session_new/continuation/resume`,
  `terminal_attach`, `open_terminal_channel`). Its `TerminalChannel` yields
  raw frames; the TUI decodes and applies them in `attach.rs`.
- `corral-tui`: today's reference client. `presentation.rs` maps a session
  row to the five-state label and secondary facts (`MainState`,
  `SessionPresentation`, `present_at`); `list.rs` decodes the list's
  loosely typed rows (`SessionListItem`) and polls every second;
  `launch.rs` runs the continuation disclosure. All of this is client-side
  vocabulary the Desktop needs identically.
- `scripts/check-dependency-direction` already names `corral-desktop` as a
  client-side crate that consumes `corral-protocol` and never `corral-core`.
- The ledger §8 decisions and the spike's measured shape: entity per
  terminal (`AnyView::cached`), custom Element positioned per wide cell,
  4 ms coalescing, tokio on its own thread bridged by a futures channel,
  qwertty-term-vt as the replica (grill Q3), `gpui = "=0.2.2"` (Q2).
- ADR 0017: the snapshot prefix a replica client must honour; the client
  rules it leaves to this plan — desync on a missing prefix, stale-epoch
  discard.

## Design

### D1 — Shared client vocabulary moves out of the TUI

`corral-client` gains `sessions` (the typed decoding of a session-list row
the TUI's `SessionListItem` is today, tolerant of unknown fields),
`presentation` (`MainState`, `SessionPresentation`, `present_at`, the
short id), and the shared launch-form vocabulary: the argument input model,
UX-side preflight helpers, and the typed rendering of the daemon's
validation errors. Not the provider grammar's authority: `session.new` is
revalidated by corrald against ADR 0012 whatever a client checked, and no
client's acceptance is ever the safety boundary (round 2, Q8). `corral-tui` re-exports and uses them; its behaviour and
snapshot tests are unchanged. One copy of the terminology law
(`PRODUCT.md` §8) for every surface, in the crate every surface already
depends on. The Desktop never depends on `corral-tui`. The boundary, as
ruled: `corral-client::presentation` may interpret daemon facts into the
accepted product presentation vocabulary; it may not own view layout,
GPUI or TUI widgets, or any independent semantic inference — daemon truth
→ shared projection vocabulary → each surface's rendering.

### D2 — The crate

`crates/corral-desktop`, `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`, workspace lints,
`publish = false`; dependencies `gpui = "=0.2.2"`, `corral-client`,
`corral-protocol`, `qwertty-term-vt`, `tokio`, `futures`, `uuid`. No
gpui-component in PR9: the list, the session view and a disclosure dialog
are a few `div`s, and the deny policy admitted gpui's tree, not another 237
crates. PR9 has not earned that dependency; a later Desktop with forms,
menus or complex reusable widgets reopens the question with real need. A
binary `corral-desktop`; bundling is M1 completion.

Everything that can be tested without a window lives in plain modules:

- `replica.rs` — the terminal state machine. Consumes decoded frames in
  order: `Geometry` and `Palette` are held until their `Snapshot` and
  applied only when the stamps agree (one state point); a `Snapshot`
  without its `Geometry` under the advertised capability is a desync — the
  snapshot is not installed and a `ResyncRequest` is sent; prefix members
  and snapshots for an epoch the replica has left are discarded. The
  parser is applied inside a poison boundary: a panic destroys the replica,
  never the process; the view shows *Terminal unavailable* and one
  automatic resync is requested; if the rebuilt replica fails again in the
  same episode, automatic retry stops until a new epoch arrives or the
  person opens the session again (grill Q3's bounded loop). Input is
  encoded from the replica's own modes (DECCKM, bracketed paste); the wire
  stays dumb.
- `bridge.rs` — a tokio runtime on its own thread; per attached session a
  reader task decoding frames into a futures channel and a writer task for
  Input/Resize/ResyncRequest; activation and reconnection through
  `corral-client` with the TUI's backoff shape — the same activation
  contract as the CLI and TUI (ADR 0001: connect to the canonical daemon,
  start it only where activation is permitted), no Desktop-side discovery,
  endpoint choice, or lifetime ownership; closing a window or quitting the
  application disconnects Desktop clients and never stops corrald, whose
  exit stays its own idle lifecycle's (round 2, Q7). Hello capabilities
  recorded so `replica` knows what the daemon promised.
- `sessions.rs` — the list model: `session_list` and `attention_summary`
  polled every second as the TUI does (a push channel is a protocol
  addition and not PR9's), rows presented through `corral-client::presentation`,
  runtime truth shown beside a degraded semantic state, never the reverse.
  The two are separate RPCs and never pose as one atomic snapshot: a poll
  generation fetches both and publishes them together only when both
  succeeded, never a fresh half beside an arbitrarily stale one. The
  daemon's summary is authoritative; the Desktop never recomputes global
  counts from visible rows. One poll in flight at a time; an immediate
  refresh after a local state-changing action or a return from Open.
- `actions.rs` — New Session: provider (Claude Code or Codex), an
  explicit working directory, and optional provider arguments under ADR
  0012's grammar with the shared preflight above and the daemon's final
  say. **No raw command, custom executable, or shell command**: `corral
  new -- <cmd>` is PR3's walking-skeleton harness, not a product promise
  that Corral manages arbitrary programs; a Custom Agent, if ever wanted,
  gets its own identity, attention, presentation, continuation, and support
  decisions first (round 2, Q8). Open/Attach
  (`terminal_attach` then the channel), input, interrupt (the accepted
  terminal input representation of Ctrl-C, ETX, as an `Input` frame — no
  new RPC, and not a function of replica modes; ordinary keys are what the
  modes encode), detach (close the channel; the run lives on), Continue
  in Corral (`session_continuation` preflight, the disclosure rendered
  before `session_resume`, exactly the TUI's `launch.rs` semantics),
  Acknowledge (`attention_acknowledge`). Each action names the capability
  it needs and is absent, not disabled, when the hello lacks it.

### D3 — Views

- `app.rs` — `Application`, one main window: the session list on the left,
  the selected session on the right (facts from `SessionPresentation`, the
  actions its state and capabilities allow, the embedded terminal).
- `terminal_element.rs` — the spike's Element: rows shaped as segments of
  consecutive narrow cells with style runs, each wide cell its own segment
  at `col × cell_width`; backgrounds and cursor as quads; 4 ms coalescing of
  notifies; the view wrapped `cached` so an idle terminal costs nothing per
  frame. Standalone mode: the same element in a window of its own, opened
  from the session view.
- **One Desktop-owned attachment per session.** PR3 froze multi-viewer
  semantics — shared geometry, last explicit resize wins — and PR9 does
  not enter them from inside one process. A `SessionTerminalEntity` owns
  the channel, the replica, and the geometry the Desktop asks for, and has
  one presentation host at a time: embedded, or standalone. "Open in
  window" moves the entity to the standalone host and leaves the main
  window a placeholder; closing the window returns it. If reparenting
  proves awkward in gpui, the standalone host renders and the embedded
  one stops, still over the single attachment. A second `terminal.attach`
  from the same Desktop for the same session is not made; two Desktop
  views of one session at once is a later, explicit entry into the
  existing multi-viewer contract.
- **Resize** (round 2, Q10): the Desktop sends `Resize` only for a local
  change of its terminal view's cell grid — pixels are recomputed into
  rows and columns, and only a changed grid is sent, coalesced across
  window-resize churn but delivered promptly at rest. A `Geometry` frame,
  a snapshot's size, an epoch change, or another viewer's resize are
  observations of daemon truth and never echo back as a resize. Moving the
  entity between the embedded and standalone hosts is a local change and
  may send one `Resize`.
- **Not in PR9** (round 2, Q11): terminal text selection, selection
  highlighting, terminal-to-clipboard copy, rectangular or link selection.
  Paste stays, being input. Stated as a limitation in the session view so
  nobody reads it as broken.
- `disclosure.rs` — the continuation disclosure, blocking until answered.

### D4 — Platform and verification envelope

macOS is the validated Desktop; the Metal toolchain is a build-time
requirement the dev-loop notes name. Linux: the crate compiles and its
non-rendering tests run in CI; the ubuntu runner installs
`libxkbcommon-dev libxkbcommon-x11-dev libwayland-dev libx11-xcb-dev
libfontconfig1-dev` as environment setup, not as verification logic. The
verification budget grows by gpui's compile; recorded as
`VERIFY_BUDGET_DEBT` if it crosses the map's threshold. A green Linux CI
never removes *unvalidated* from the support matrix: if M1 later claims
Linux Desktop support, real display, render, and input validation on
Linux is mandatory before that claim.

## Interfaces or persistence changed

No wire change, no durable state. `corral-client` gains two public
modules; `corral-tui` loses two private ones. `Cargo.toml` gains a member
and the gpui pin.

## Failure / unknown states

- Daemon unreachable: the list keeps the last facts as historical context
  with their age and the activation error in the TUI's words; a cached row
  is not a current semantic claim — the freshness law applies as it does
  anywhere: a `Needs You` the daemon last asserted rots to Unknown on its
  own horizon whether or not the Desktop is connected, and caching never
  extends it. Actions are absent; a terminal channel that ends shows
  *Detached* with the reason frame if one came.
- Snapshot prefix missing or stamped apart: not installed; resync.
- Replica poisoned: replica destroyed, *Terminal unavailable*, one resync;
  then stop; session and daemon truth untouched.
- Continuation refused: the refusal code shown; nothing launched.
- Unsupported or limited-awareness sessions: rendered as `PRODUCT.md` §6
  says, provably safe actions only.

## Tests

- `replica` (unit, in-crate): prefix order and stamps, desync on a missing
  `Geometry`, stale-epoch discard, palette checkpoint applied before the
  snapshot, the failure episode (poison → unavailable → one resync → stop;
  re-armed only by re-open or a daemon-produced epoch), the legacy-geometry
  rules, input encoding per mode.
- `bridge` and the wire (round 2, Q12): against a real **test-support
  `corrald` binary** launched under an isolated test root, as the `corral`
  crate's e2e does — never a dev-dependency on `corrald`, whose internals
  no client crate links even for tests. The harness inherits the e2e
  isolation contract in full: a validated test-build binary (and any
  `corral` helper), an explicit test root, failure before spawning if the
  test-build marker or isolation contract is missing, never the canonical
  endpoint or `~/.corral`, and correctness while another shell builds
  `corral` or `corrald` concurrently — a wrong binary is a test failure,
  never a production activation. Crossed only where the public client
  contract is under test: hello and capabilities, attach, the
  `Geometry/Palette/Snapshot` prefix, resync, channel termination and
  reconnect, compatibility behaviour; corrald's own fidelity suite is not
  duplicated from the client side.
- `sessions`/`actions`: decoding fixtures with unknown fields and
  discriminants; every action gated by capability; the disclosure shown
  before a resume.
- Views: `gpui` test-support (`TestAppContext`) for list and selection
  logic; no pixel assertions.
- `corral-tui`: its existing snapshot tests prove D1 changed nothing.

## Definition of done

- The core loop through the Desktop: see every session with its state,
  know what needs you (counts and rows), take control (open, type,
  interrupt, continue). Manually walked on macOS and recorded in the PR.
- The spike's frame harness rerun in **release profile** under a real
  display link on the Desktop's element: paint p95 within the 8 ms budget
  at 200×60, recorded (grill Q8/Q12). **Measured** on the release element
  (`cargo run --release -p corral-desktop --example frame_harness --
  --size 60x200`, macOS 26.5, CVDisplayLink at ~60 Hz, an ~9.4 MB/s
  storm): paint p95 **0.91–0.95 ms** at 200×60 over two 10 s runs (max
  6.0 ms, the cold first frame), and **0.20 ms** at 80×24 — the real
  element a touch faster than the spike's throwaway harness (1.34 ms) and
  seven times inside the budget. Arrival-to-paint stays vsync-bound at
  10–18 ms p50.
- `./scripts/verify` on macOS and the Linux CI both green; the support
  matrix line for Linux Desktop reads *unvalidated*.
- ROADMAP §3's PR9 line points at this plan; the ledger's §8 row cites the
  release numbers.

## Decisions ruled 2026-09-05 (`docs/decisions/2026-09-05-pr9-plan-grill.md`)

1. D1, the lift into `corral-client` — accepted, with the boundary stated.
2. D2, no gpui-component in PR9 — accepted; not earned yet, not never.
3. 1 Hz polling, no push — accepted, with the poll-generation rule.
4. One automatic resync per failure episode — accepted with the re-arm
   constraint.
5. One main window, standalone on demand — accepted with one
   Desktop-owned attachment per session.
6. Linux compile and tests only — accepted; the support gate stands.

Round 2 (same record): Q7 activation as CLI/TUI, Desktop never a daemon
lifecycle authority; Q8 New Session is Claude or Codex + working directory
+ provider arguments, no raw command, validation authority stays in
corrald; Q9 the keyboard set above, no mouse reporting; Q10 the resize
rule; Q11 no selection/copy; Q12 test topology B with e2e isolation
inherited; Q13 legacy geometry fallback, silent, forbidden once the
capability is advertised.

## Plan Size Justification

Over the target because PR9 is the largest single phase in the schedule
(`2026-08-22-surface-sequencing.md`) and this is its one plan: one crate,
one lift out of the TUI, and the client half of ADR 0017's contract. The
pieces share one owner boundary — the Desktop surface — and splitting them
would ship a Desktop that cannot yet render a terminal, or a replica with
no surface to prove it.
